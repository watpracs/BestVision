Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent guidelines for contributions, accidental transactions, and refund requests made to Best Vision Foundation.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Non-Profit Organization Policy Notice

Best Vision Foundation (BVF) is a legally registered non-profit organization under the NGO Secretariat of Sri Lanka (Reg: L-184510). Payments collected through this website are voluntary donations and sponsorships to fund conservation operations and public welfare missions.

1. Nature of Contributions

Because Best Vision Foundation is a non-governmental charitable foundation, payments made through our website are voluntary, non-commercial gifts and donations. As these funds are quickly allocated to time-sensitive emergency animal surgeries, veterinary medicine, field fuel, and coastal cleanup operations, donations are generally non-refundable once committed.

2. Exceptional Circumstances for Refunds

We recognize that genuine errors occasionally happen. Best Vision Foundation will gladly examine and process a full refund in the following exceptional situations:

  • Duplicate Charges: You were charged more than once for a single donation due to a technical glitch, network delay, or multiple clicks.
  • Erroneous Amount: You entered an unintended donation amount (for example, typing LKR 50,000 instead of LKR 5,000) and notify us promptly.
  • Unauthorized Card Usage: The donation was made fraudulently using your lost or stolen credit/debit card without your permission.

3. Cancellation Policy (Recurring & Pledged Contributions)

Donors who commit to periodic support or recurring monthly contributions may cancel their donation schedule at any time without fees, penalties, or obligations:

  • Notice Period: Cancellation requests should be submitted at least 24 hours before the scheduled deduction or billing date.
  • How to Cancel: Email our finance desk at bvfsrilanka@gmail.com with the subject line "Cancel Donation Schedule" or alert our secretariat via WhatsApp at +94 77 730 4152.
  • Execution: Your donation recurring mandate will be deactivated immediately with written email confirmation. No subsequent charges will occur.

4. Timeframe for Refund Requests

All refund requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the original transaction date. Requests made after 14 days cannot be processed, as the funds will have already been deployed into direct field conservation programs.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, please email our finance desk at bvfsrilanka@gmail.com with the subject line "Donation Refund Request", including the following details:

  • Full Name of the Donor / Cardholder
  • Contact Phone Number and Email Address
  • Transaction Date and exact Amount (with currency)
  • PayHere Payment Reference ID or Order ID (from your email receipt)
  • Reason for the refund request

You may also alert our secretariat via phone / WhatsApp at +94 77 730 4152 or +94 77 970 5752.

6. Processing Timeline & Method

Upon receiving your complete documentation, our administrative team will review the transaction within 2 to 3 business days.

  • Card Payments: Approved card refunds are reversed directly back to the original issuing credit or debit card through the PayHere gateway. Depending on your bank's processing cycles, the reversal typically reflects on your bank statement within 7 to 14 business days.
  • Bank Transfers (CEFT / Wire): Wire transfer reversals are made back to the originating bank account name after written verification.
  • No cash refunds will be provided for online card transactions under any circumstances.

7. Inquiries & Assistance

For urgent payment questions or transaction clarifications, please contact our secretariat:
