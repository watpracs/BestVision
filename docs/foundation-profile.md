## 3. Core Mission & Vision
* **Vision:** A sustainable, compassionate, and empowered Sri Lankan society where ecosystems thrive, wildlife is safeguarded, and communities live in harmony with dignity and digital safety.
* **Mission:** To mobilize grassroots collective action, foster environmental stewardship, protect endangered species and wildlife habitats, champion animal welfare, and advance social and digital literacy for vulnerable populations.

---

## 4. Key Pillars & Active Initiatives

### 1. Environmental Stewardship & Lagoon Protection
* **Negombo Lagoon Conservation Campaign:** Comprehensive clean-up drives, plastic waste reduction, mangrove habitat restoration, and waterway clearing.
* **Marine & Wetland Ecosystem Support:** Partnering with coastal artisanal fishing families to mitigate aquatic pollution, protect nursery grounds, and promote sustainable coastal ecology.

### 2. Wildlife Conservation & Animal Welfare
* **Elephant Protection & Human-Elephant Coexistence:** Working in vulnerable buffer zones to mitigate human-elephant conflicts (HEC), advocating against habitat encroachment and snares, and fostering community awareness.
* **Animal Welfare & Rescue:** Rescuing injured animals, raising veterinary care awareness, and advocating for humane treatment of stray and wild animals.

### 3. Digital Rights & Social Advocacy
* **Collective for Social Media Declaration (CSMD) Member:** Active partner within civil society coalitions advocating for ethical digital platforms, youth cyber-safety, digital inclusion, and counter-harassment mechanisms.
* **Human Rights & Civil Society Voice:** Standing in solidarity with cross-sectoral civic networks to safeguard civic space, freedom of expression, and transparent governance.

### 4. Community Empowerment & Youth Engagement
* **Grassroots Livelihood Upliftment:** Empowering local youth and women through skill workshops, eco-tourism ideas, and environmental volunteerism.
* **Public Education & Volunteer Mobilization:** Conducting workshops in schools and community centers on waste segregation, biodiversity, and ethical community leadership.
