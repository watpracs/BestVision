# Best Vision Foundation - Website Architecture & Design System

## 1. Design System & Theme

### Color Palette
* **Primary Deep Emerald:** `#0d5c46` (Representing environmental sustainability and vitality)
* **Accent Nature Green:** `#10b981` (Vibrant action green for buttons, highlights, badges)
* **Secondary Ocean / Lagoon Blue:** `#0284c7` (Negombo Lagoon, water protection, trust)
* **Warm Earth Gold / Sand:** `#eab308` / `#f59e0b` (Wildlife, elephants, warmth)
* **Background Light:** `#f8fafc` / `#ffffff`
* **Text Dark:** `#0f172a` (High legibility slate)
* **Text Muted:** `#64748b`

### Typography
* **Primary Heading:** `'Outfit', 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Body Text:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`

## 2. Information Architecture (Sitemap)

1. **Home (`index.html`)**
   * Top Alert / Notice bar (NGO Reg: L-184510)
   * Main Navigation with logo, links, social handles, and "Donate / Get Involved" CTA
   * Hero Banner with impactful headline, mission statement, quick CTAs
   * Impact Counter & Metrics (Lagoon areas restored, community workshops, wildlife advocacy initiatives)
   * Core Pillars (Lagoon Conservation, Wildlife Protection, Digital Rights / CSMD, Community Empowerment)
   * Featured Campaigns & Photo Stories
   * Interactive Volunteer & Supporter Callout
   * Testimonials & Voices from the Community
   * Latest News & Social Updates (Direct link to FB page)
   * Rich Footer with full NGO details, Google Map link, Quick Links, Newsletter

2. **About Us (`about.html`)**
   * Who We Are & Historical Roots in Negombo
   * Official NGO Registration & Transparency details
   * President's Message (Ms. Kaushalya Chamarasinghe)
   * Guiding Principles & Ethics
   * Our Partners & Coalition Affiliations (e.g., CSMD)

3. **Projects & Campaigns (`projects.html`)**
   * Filterable Project Showcase: All / Environment & Lagoon / Wildlife & Animals / Digital Rights / Community
   * Project Deep-Dives:
     - Negombo Lagoon Cleanup & Mangrove Regeneration
     - Human-Elephant Coexistence & Habitat Defense
     - Digital Rights & Responsible Social Media (CSMD)
     - Coastal Community Resilience & Artisanal Fisherfolk Support
   * Interactive modal / gallery showcase

4. **Get Involved (`get-involved.html`)**
   * Volunteer Application Form
   * How You Can Help (Cleanups, Wildlife Monitoring, Social Advocacy, Donations)
   * Transparency & Direct Support / Bank Account details for donations
   * FAQ for Volunteers and Donors

5. **Contact Us (`contact.html`)**
   * Physical Address in 3rd Kurana, Negombo
   * Direct contact numbers & email
   * Interactive Contact / Inquiry Form
   * Direct link to Facebook community page
   * Embedded interactive map / location guide

## 3. Technology Stack
* Pure Semantic Modern HTML5, Responsive CSS3 with custom variables & flex/grid, and vanilla ES6 JavaScript.
* Zero external heavy dependencies required, fast loading, mobile-first, and 100% ready for hosting on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or cPanel.
* Includes FontAwesome / Google Fonts via CDN for crisp icons and typography.
