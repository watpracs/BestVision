Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How Best Vision Foundation protects donor information, personal records, and financial privacy.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Commitment to Donor Privacy

Best Vision Foundation (BVF) (NGO Reg: L-184510) respects the privacy of all donors, volunteers, and website visitors. We adhere to the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka and global best practices for charitable transparency.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, make a donation, register as a volunteer, or contact our secretariat, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, telephone / WhatsApp number, postal address, and city.
  • Donation Details: Contribution amount, transaction date, payment reference, and chosen project allocation (e.g. Elephant Hospital or Lagoon Cleanup).
  • Volunteer Information: Skills, availability, and focus area interests submitted via our volunteer intake form.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically through standard server logs for site security.

2. Payment Security & Card Data Protection

Best Vision Foundation DOES NOT capture, view, or store credit or debit card numbers, CVV codes, or online banking passwords on our servers.

All card transactions are processed securely through PayHere, an authorized Payment Service Provider regulated by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka and certified under global PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1 compliance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data exclusively for non-profit and charitable operational purposes:

  • To process your online donation and generate official charitable payment acknowledgments and receipts.
  • To coordinate field volunteer missions, cleanup schedules, and orientation briefings.
  • To provide milestone updates and field reports regarding the specific wildlife or environmental project you funded.
  • To fulfill mandatory financial reporting and statutory audits required by the National NGO Secretariat of Sri Lanka.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, trade, or commercialize your personal information to third parties.

Your data is only shared with verified institutional service partners who assist us in operating our website and processing payments (e.g. PayHere and Sampath Bank), or when strictly required by Sri Lankan regulatory authorities under lawful judicial orders.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses minimal, essential browser storage (such as light/dark mode theme preference) to provide an optimal browsing experience. We do not use intrusive commercial ad-tracking cookies or third-party behavioral profiling scripts.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act of Sri Lanka, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate details.
  • Request deletion of your contact records from our volunteer or newsletter distribution lists.

7. Data Protection Inquiries

If you have questions about our data practices or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please contact our secretariat:
