2. Charitable Purpose & Non-Profit Nature

Best Vision Foundation operates exclusively as a charitable, non-profit organization focused on:

  • Marine and wetland preservation, plastic and debris interception in Negombo Lagoon and coastal areas.
  • Endangered Asian elephant protection, safe wildlife corridors, and human-elephant coexistence initiatives.
  • Island-wide stray animal welfare, emergency field rescue, medical rehabilitation, and safe shelter.
  • Coastal community education, sustainable livelihood support, and digital literacy.

All funds and contributions accepted through this website represent voluntary non-profit donations and charitable sponsorships dedicated exclusively to furthering these philanthropic missions.

3. Online Donations & Payment Processing

When contributing online via credit/debit card, digital wallet, or online bank transfer:

  • Payment Gateway: All card payments and electronic transactions are securely handled by PayHere (licensed and regulated as a Payment Service Provider by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka) or direct CEFT bank transfers to our official Sampath Bank account.
  • Security: Best Vision Foundation does not capture, store, or process credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers. All sensitive financial data is encrypted and processed via PCI-DSS compliant secure protocols.
  • Currencies & Fees: Donations may be presented in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) or United States Dollars (USD). Foreign card contributions are settled according to applicable commercial bank exchange rates.
  • Authorization: By submitting a donation, you certify that you are authorized to use the chosen payment instrument and that all provided personal details are authentic and accurate.

4. Allocation of Contributions

Donors may earmark their contributions to specific conservation projects (such as the Elephant Hospital, Stray Dog Shelter, or Negombo Lagoon Cleanup). Best Vision Foundation makes every reasonable effort to apply earmarked funds directly to the designated project. If a project reaches full funding or operational conditions necessitate reallocations, funds are redirected to the closest aligned conservation priority under oversight of the Foundation’s Board of Trustees.
