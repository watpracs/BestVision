Frequently Asked Questions

Who can volunteer?

Anyone over 16 with a passion for wildlife, wetlands, or community justice. Students and working professionals are all welcome!

Where do cleanups take place?

Mainly along Negombo Lagoon estuaries, beaches, and canal feeder choke points. Field schedules are published on our Facebook page.

Can corporations partner for CSR?

Yes, we regularly structure impactful corporate environmental CSR cleanups, mangrove plantings, and digital literacy initiatives.
