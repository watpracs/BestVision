Establishment of the First Elephant Hospital, Rescue Center & Orphanage in North Central Province, Sri Lanka

Strategic Danger Zone: Habarana / Minneriya / Anuradhapura • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (BVF)

The Crisis on the Ground

Elephants in North Central Province suffer catastrophic mortalities daily due to high-speed railway accidents, jaw-shattering Hakka Patas explosives, agricultural trap guns, and territorial conflict. The nearest specialized veterinary facility is 250 km away, and almost all heavily wounded elephants succumb during prolonged road transit. Building this permanent facility in Habarana / Minneriya directly solves this emergency.

Official Marine Ecology Proposal Total Project Budget: $97,000 USD

Removal and Recovery of Abandoned & Sunken Vessels, Ghost Nets, and Marine Debris in Negombo Lagoon

Target Area: Negombo Estuary, Thalahena, Munnakkaraya & Pitipana • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (Reg: L-184510)

Environmental Emergency & Navigational Hazard

Negombo Lagoon is critically degraded by 50 to 80 abandoned, semi-submerged, and sunken fishing crafts alongside over 80 to 100 tons of discarded synthetic ghost nets and sunken industrial debris. These submerged hazards cause fatal boat collisions for artisanal fishermen, tear working nets, trap and kill endangered sea turtles and juvenile fish, and leach toxic motor oil and antifouling lead into the mangrove nursery ecosystem.
