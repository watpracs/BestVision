Project Blue-Green
Negombo Lagoon & Estuary Ecosystems

Lagoon Debris Clearance & Mangrove Nurseries

Negombo Lagoon supports thousands of artisanal fishing families and diverse migratory birds. BVF coordinates periodic volunteer water cleanups, extracts tons of plastic debris, and restores mangrove fringing to protect crucial fish spawning nurseries.

  • Regular boat patrols clearing toxic marine debris
  • Coastal mangrove seedling propagation & replanting
  • Direct support for low-income artisanal fishing households

Project Pachyderm
Buffer Zones & Migration Corridors

Asian Elephant Defense & Anti-Snare Advocacy

Led by Ms. Kaushalya Chamarasinghe, we fight against cruel wire snares, illegal electric fences, and train strikes, mobilizing rapid veterinary rescue interventions and promoting peaceful coexistence with farmers.

  • Rapid veterinary first-responder coordination
  • Community buffer-zone safety workshops
  • Public legal advocacy against snare cruelty

President's Address

"With a vision to uplift and empower communities across Sri Lanka, I established Best Vision Foundation to create lasting positive change through welfare, education, humanitarian initiatives, and wildlife and animal protection."

A core part of our mission is dedicated to wildlife conservation and animal welfare — protecting endangered species, rescuing injured animals, developing rural schools, uplifting families in poverty, and safeguarding wild elephants in danger zones. Together, we can build a stronger, kinder society for both people and animals.
