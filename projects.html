Active Field Missions & Campaigns

Real-world operations designed to halt ecological degradation, protect endangered wildlife, and defend civic freedom.

Sector 01: Marine & Wetland
Negombo Coastal Lagoon & Mangrove Nursery

Negombo Lagoon Plastic Interception & Mangrove Regeneration

Negombo Lagoon is an irreplaceable ecological lifeline supporting thousands of traditional fishing families. BVF coordinates regular debris cleanups, plastic interception drives, and community-led mangrove seedling replanting to protect fish breeding nurseries.

Impact Benchmark: 18+ comprehensive sweeps completed, multiple tons of toxic plastic debris extracted, and over 1,000 mangrove seedlings propagated.
Sector 02: Endangered Wildlife Defense

Asian Elephant Coexistence & Anti-Snare Emergency Units

Sri Lanka's Asian elephants face severe threats from habitat fragmentation, illegal electrical fences, and deadly wire snare traps. Under the leadership of Ms. Kaushalya Chamarasinghe, BVF advocates against cruel traps and aids injured wildlife.

Impact Benchmark: Rapid coordination with wildlife veterinarians, active buffer-zone farmer education, and zero-tolerance legal advocacy against snares.

Sector 03: Digital Rights
Islandwide Coalition / CSMD Partner

Collective for Social Media Declaration (CSMD) & Digital Safety

As an active signatory and civil society partner in the CSMD coalition, BVF advocates for democratic online environments, safeguarding citizens against digital surveillance, cyberbullying, hate campaigns, and unfair digital legislation.

Impact Benchmark: Co-signatory on major national civil society statements on online safety bills, youth cyber-hygiene, and digital freedom.

Sector 04: Community Resilience

Artisanal Fisherfolk Livelihoods & Youth Eco-Clubs

Supporting coastal artisanal families facing resource depletion and economic volatility. We run educational awareness programs in local schools to foster a generation of environmentally conscious community leaders.

Impact Benchmark: Mentored youth environmental volunteer groups, organized waste-segregation literacy workshops, and supported coastal schools.
