Official Project Dossier Total Capital Setup: $730,950 USD • Annual Ops: $184,920 USD

Establishment of the First Elephant Emergency Hospital, Rescue Center & Orphanage in North Central Province

Target Location: Habarana / Minneriya / Anuradhapura • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (Reg: L-184510)

The 250 km Emergency Gap

Elephants in the North Central Province suffer from train accidents, Hakka Patas (explosive bait), gunshot wounds, and cruel wire snares. The nearest specialized wildlife hospital is over 250 km away in Udawalawa. Over 70% of critically injured elephants die during transit. BVF is building the first fully equipped trauma center right inside the danger corridor.

Island-Wide Stray Dog Rescue, Medical Treatment & Safe Shelter Project

Target Area: Western & Southern Provinces • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (Reg: L-184510)

Ground Reality & Urgent Appeal

Hundreds of stray dogs in Sri Lankan towns are suffering from severe skin mange, bacterial ulcers, lice, ticks, fleas, and hit-and-run fractures. Without shelter, they endure heavy monsoon rains and starvation. BVF provides emergency roadside rescue, professional clinical surgery, anti-rabies vaccination, internal de-worming, nutrition, and compassionate adoption pathways.

Official Marine Ecology Proposal Total Project Budget: $97,000 USD

Removal and Recovery of Abandoned & Sunken Vessels, Ghost Nets, and Marine Debris in Negombo Lagoon

Target Area: Negombo Estuary, Thalahena, Munnakkaraya & Pitipana • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (Reg: L-184510)

Environmental Emergency & Navigational Hazard

Negombo Lagoon is critically degraded by 50 to 80 abandoned, semi-submerged, and sunken fishing crafts alongside over 80 to 100 tons of discarded synthetic ghost nets and sunken industrial debris. These submerged hazards cause fatal boat collisions for artisanal fishermen, tear working nets, trap and kill endangered sea turtles and juvenile fish, and leach toxic motor oil and antifouling lead into the mangrove nursery ecosystem.
