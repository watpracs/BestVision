Establishment of the First Elephant Hospital, Rescue Center & Orphanage in North Central Province, Sri Lanka

Strategic Danger Zone: Habarana / Minneriya / Anuradhapura • Initiated By: Best Vision Foundation (BVF)

The Crisis on the Ground

Elephants in North Central Province suffer catastrophic mortalities daily due to high-speed railway accidents, jaw-shattering Hakka Patas explosives, agricultural trap guns, and territorial conflict. The nearest specialized veterinary facility is 250 km away, and almost all heavily wounded elephants succumb during prolonged road transit. Building this permanent facility in Habarana / Minneriya directly solves this emergency.
