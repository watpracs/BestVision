BEST VISION FOUNDATION – SRI LANKA

"We kindly request all animal-loving people around the world to join hands with Best Vision Foundation from Sri Lanka to safeguard elephants living in danger zones in Sri Lanka."

Annually, 350 to 400 baby elephants and pregnant mother elephants die due to tragic railway accidents, agricultural conflict, trap guns, and deliberate poisoning.

We are committed to protecting them and providing a peaceful environment in Sri Lanka. If we take all possible protection measures now, our future generations will be able to see them living in peaceful habitats. Otherwise, future generations will only see them in videos, like dinosaurs.

Project Roadmap

Pending & Proposed Projects

Our pending initiatives for the development of the Sri Lankan community, wildlife sectors, and coastal ecosystems. Help us launch them early with your generous donation.

#01 Priority Pending

Clearing of Debris in Lagoons and Tanks

Organizing systematic debris extraction across Negombo, Chilaw, and interior dry zone water tanks to clear suffocating plastic waste, industrial refuse, and siltation to restore natural aquatic ecosystems.

Waterways & Tanks
#02 Priority Pending

Providing Continuous Food Supply to Monkey Island in Negombo

Establishing a reliable, nutritious dietary replenishment protocol for native primates inhabiting Monkey Island in Negombo, preventing starvation, nutritional stress, and destructive human scavenging.

Wildlife Habitat
#03 Priority Pending

Continuous Cleaning Program for All Waterways in the Island

Islandwide network mobilization engaging grassroots teams to continually clean river mouths, canal networks, and estuary streams, stopping toxic plastic before it reaches ocean waters.

Islandwide Waterways
#04 Priority Pending

Providing Fishing Gear to Underprivileged Fishing Families

Supplying essential eco-friendly nets, navigational safety equipment, and gear to impoverished coastal artisanal fishermen unable to afford equipment, lifting their families from generational poverty.

Fisherfolk Livelihood
#05 Priority Pending

Providing Necessary Facilities to Underprivileged Rural Schools

Supplying clean drinking water filtration systems, proper sanitation units, essential classroom furniture, and learning materials to neglected remote schools across marginalized rural districts.

Rural Education
#06 Priority Pending

Supporting Poor Children in Education with Career-Long Follow-up

A comprehensive scholarship and mentorship program that financially and academically sponsors underprivileged students from primary grades until completion of their final school career.

Long-Term Sponsorship
#07 Priority Pending

Material & Machinery Support to Skilled Workers in Need

Equipping skilled carpenters, mechanics, welders, and artisans with the vital machinery and tools they cannot afford, unlocking sustainable self-employment and dignified local livelihoods.

Artisan Empowerment
#08 Priority Pending

Construction & Proper Maintenance of Public Toilet Facilities

Building hygienic, universally accessible public sanitary facilities in bustling town centers and markets with an institutional ongoing maintenance framework to prevent health hazards.

Public Sanitation
#09 High Urgency

Safeguarding Elephants in Danger Zones via Fences & Railway Corridors

Installing community bio-fences (lime and palmyra cultivation), electric buffer boundaries, and engineered railway pathway corridors at high-risk collision tracks to save elephants from gruesome deaths.

Elephant Corridors
#10 High Urgency

Elephant Medical Center & Orphanage for Deadly Injuries

Introducing a specialized veterinary field clinic and treatment orphanage to rescue, rehabilitate, and administer surgical care to wild elephants wounded by jaw-bombs (Hakka Patas), traps, and gunshots.

Veterinary Sanctuary
#11 New Proposed

Mangrove Replanting in Negombo, Chilaw & Puttalam Lagoons

Restoring vital coastal nurseries by cultivating native Rhizophora mangrove saplings across eroded lagoons, defending biodiversity and bolstering fish and shrimp breeding grounds.

Mangrove Restoration
#12 New Proposed

Coral Reef Restoration & Ghost Net Removal

Deploying diver squads across Trincomalee, Hikkaduwa, and Negombo coastal seas to extract suffocating discarded ghost fishing nets and transplant climate-resilient coral micro-fragments.

Marine Conservation
#13 New Proposed

Sea Turtle Hatchery Protection & Anti-Poaching Patrols

Guarding vulnerable nesting beaches, intercepting illegal egg poaching, and running community-supervised turtle hatcheries to safely release thousands of endangered hatchlings into the ocean.

Turtle Protection
#14 New Proposed

Stray Animal Sterilization & Rabies Vaccination Program

Mobile humane veterinary clinics administering CNVR (Catch, Neuter, Vaccinate, Release) across municipal and coastal areas to compassionately control the stray dog population and eradicate rabies.

Animal Welfare
#15 New Proposed

E-Waste and Hazardous Battery Collection Program

Setting up dedicated drop-off points for electronics and battery recycling to stop lead, mercury, and cadmium contaminants from leaching into drinking water aquifers and lagoon estuaries.

Toxic Waste Interception
#16 New Proposed

Rainwater Harvesting Systems for Dry Zone Villages

Installing affordable, robust rooftop rainwater harvesting tanks in drought-prone hamlets across Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, and Hambantota to prevent Chronic Kidney Disease (CKDu).

Clean Water Access
#17 New Proposed

Organic Home Gardening Training for Poor Families

Distributing heirloom seeds, composting kits, and agricultural training to low-income households to guarantee daily household nutrition and self-sufficiency amid inflation.

Food Sovereignty
#18 New Proposed

Life Jacket Bank for Small-Boat Artisanal Fishermen

Creating an accessible community revolving safety depository providing ISO-certified life jackets on free loan to small traditional non-motorized craft fishermen, averting open-sea drownings.

Maritime Safety
#19 New Proposed

Vocational Training for Fishermen's Children in Marine Tech

Free technical academy training youth in outboard boat engine mechanics, fiberglass vessel maintenance, marine electrical systems, and GPS satellite navigation for modern maritime careers.

Youth Skills
#20 New Proposed

Beach & Public Market Cleaning with Women's Collectives

A dual-impact ecological model hiring vulnerable female heads-of-household for paid beach and municipal fish market sanitization, providing dignified daily incomes while cleaning urban habitats.

Women Empowerment
