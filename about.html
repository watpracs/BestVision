About Best Vision Foundation

Dedicated to environmental resilience, wildlife protection, and citizen advocacy from Negombo to across Sri Lanka.

Our Journey & Mandate

Rooted in Kurana, Serving Sri Lanka

The Best Vision Foundation (BVF) was founded with a direct mission: to confront acute ecological crises and social vulnerabilities at the community level. Based in Kurana, Katunayake, the foundation is officially registered under the National Secretariat for Non-Governmental Organizations with Registration No: L-184510.

From restoring degraded mangrove tracts and conducting volunteer debris removals in the Negombo Lagoon to mitigating the human-elephant conflict in critical border corridors, BVF combines direct field work with proactive civil advocacy.

In addition to environmental conservation, the foundation actively defends civic space and digital rights as an engaged member of the Collective for Social Media Declaration (CSMD), championing online safety, freedom of expression, and accountability.

Guiding Compass

Vision, Mission & Core Principles

Our Vision

A compassionate, resilient, and environmentally sustainable Sri Lanka where rich biodiversity is safeguarded, vulnerable human communities flourish with dignity, and digital spaces remain ethical and safe for all generations.

Our Mission

To mobilize direct grassroots participation, conduct evidence-based wetland and wildlife preservation, support marginalized coastal fisherfolk, and advocate steadfastly for justice, animal welfare, and digital rights.

Core Values

  • Grassroots Action & Direct Field Impact
  • Environmental Ethics & Biodiversity Respect
  • Civic Courage & Transparent Stewardship
  • Inclusivity & Youth Leadership
